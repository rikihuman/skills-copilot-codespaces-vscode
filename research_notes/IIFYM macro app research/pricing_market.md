# Pricing and Business Models of Macro/Nutrition Tracking and Diet Coaching Apps (as of Nov 2026)

Research method note: Research was done on 2026-11-08. In this session, direct page fetches to apps.apple.com, revenuecat.com and help.macrofactorapp.com were blocked by the sandbox network policy, so almost all figures below come from web-search snippets of primary pages (App Store listings, vendor help pages) or from secondary aggregators and competitor blogs (Nutrola, NutriScan, Subger, App Pricing Lab). Aggregator and competitor-blog figures often conflict. Treat every price as "reported, not live-verified," and check the stores before publishing. All prices are USD (US storefront) unless stated otherwise.

## Q1. Current pricing and tiers of competitor apps (comparison table)

### Takeaway
Software-only macro trackers cluster at about $60–100/yr ($9–20/mo). "Coach" apps that adjust macros algorithmically (Carbon, RP Diet, Avatar) run about $80–150/yr ($10–20/mo). Workout-plus-diet bundles and premium "Plus" tiers reach about $100–300/yr. Noom-style behavioural programmes reach about $199+/yr. AI photo-logging apps use aggressive dynamic pricing from about $30 to $150/yr.

### Inferences
- There are three clear US price bands: (1) commodity trackers at $40–60/yr (Cronometer, FatSecret, MyNetDiary, Yazio, Lose It); (2) "smart/adaptive coach" apps at $72–100/yr (MacroFactor, Carbon, Avatar, MFP Premium/Premium+); (3) premium specialist apps at $150–300/yr (RP Diet top tier, SnapCalorie list price, RP Hypertrophy, Noom).
- Monthly plans cost about 2–2.5x the annual plan on a per-month basis (for example MacroFactor $11.99/mo vs $6/mo annualised, and MFP $19.99 vs $6.67). The industry steers users to annual plans.
- MyFitnessPal's acquisition of Cal AI suggests incumbents treat AI photo logging as a separate, cheaper, mass-market funnel ($30–40/yr) rather than a premium feature.
- Feature differentiators (from background knowledge, NOT verified this session): MacroFactor, Carbon, RP Diet and Avatar all sell adaptive TDEE or algorithmic macro adjustment as the core paid value. MyFitnessPal paywalls barcode scanning, while MacroFactor and Cronometer include it. Photo logging is spreading across most apps as an AI feature.

### Gaps
- No verified pricing found for MyMacros+, Macros First, Fitbod (training-only comparator), Mealime, Trainerize-based coach pricing, or The Macro Experiment's own coaching/programme. These searches were not reached within the tool budget, or returned nothing.
- Trial lengths were mostly not verifiable; only Avatar's 2-week trial was found. Lifetime options were found only for MyNetDiary.
- No live App Store reads were possible, so no prices could be confirmed first-hand. The Carbon, Lose It and Cronometer conflicts remain unresolved.

## Q2. South African (ZAR) pricing and local competitors

### Takeaway
Hardly any verifiable ZAR prices were found. The only data point is an aggregator's capture of MyFitnessPal in the South African App Store (Premium about R339.99/mo, R1,399.99/yr), which suggests major apps price South Africa near USD parity rather than with a PPP discount. No Discovery/Vitality-branded calorie or macro tracker was found. Local human macro coaching starts at about R750/mo.

### Cited Findings
- MyFitnessPal in the South African App Store: "Premium" R339.99, "Monthly Premium" R349.99, "Yearly Premium" R1,399.99. The capture date is unknown and the listing is internally inconsistent — [AppTail ZA](https://apptail.io/app/myfitnesspal-calorie-counter-Zr/south-africa)
- Discovery Vitality awards points for wearable and health-app data (steps, heart rate, sleep). No Discovery-owned nutrition/macro-logging app was found — [Discovery Vitality FAQ](https://www.discovery.co.za/vitality/science-of-vitality-hub); [Vitality Active Device benefit guide](https://discovery.co.za/wcm/discoverycoza/assets/vitality/benefit-guides/vitality-active-device-benefit-guide.pdf)
- An app called "Vitality: Fitness & Nutrition" is a third-party app (© Kapil Lamba), not Discovery's, priced at $8.99/yr or $0.99/mo — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6749547421)
- Apple's Aug 2021 South Africa (ZAR) price sheet maps tier 6 to R99.99. Tiers are country-specific, so R99.99 is not equal to a USD tier — [Apple partner PDF](https://itunespartner.apple.com/assets/downloads/072821-Apps-Pricing-SAfr-UK-EUR.pdf/)
- Apple has overhauled App Store pricing (prices up to R175,000 are now possible) and added rounded price points such as $10 instead of $9.99, starting with subscriptions — [TechCentral](https://techcentral.co.za/apple-overhauls-app-store-pricing-apps-costing-r175-000-now-possible/218308/)
- Historically, Apple has adjusted SA store prices for rand moves and 15% VAT (for example, rises when the rand fell and a 2023 increase) — [TechCentral](https://techcentral.co.za/falling-rand-prompts-app-store-price-hike/194146); [Nairametrics 2023](https://nairametrics.com/2023/01/28/iphone-users-in-nigeria-south-africa-others-to-pay-more-for-apps)
- A price-localisation guide lists South Africa at ZAR 163.49 for a $19.99 base price. That implies a PPP-style discount of roughly 55–60% versus straight FX conversion — [PricePush cheat sheet](https://pricepush.app/blog/app-price-localization-cheat-sheet)
- The 146 Method, a South African meal-prep and macro coaching service, charges R750/mo for a custom meal plan, consultation and macro ranges — [The 146 Method](https://the146.vercel.app/meal-plans). Leanliving.co.za sells "transformation packages" (price not captured) — [Lean Living](https://www.leanliving.co.za/products/ultimate-transformation-package)
- South African online life-coaching sessions run R850–1,600 per hour (May 2025) — [rate card](https://sites.google.com/view/lifecoachconsulting/rate-card)

### Inferences
- Assumption: the exchange rate is about R17–18.5 per USD in 2026 (not verified this session). On that basis, MyFitnessPal's R1,399.99/yr is about $76–82, which is close to its US $79.99. Large incumbents appear to charge SA users near US parity.
- A PPP-adjusted entrant could undercut incumbents in ZAR substantially. For example, R99–149/mo or R699–999/yr would be about 40–60% below MFP Premium in ZAR, while still roughly matching the PPP-adjusted level in the localisation guide.
- Discovery Vitality is a distribution and partnership opportunity rather than a competitor. It rewards wearable and app data, so a nutrition app integrating with Vitality-supported data flows (for example Apple Health, Google Fit, Garmin) could be attractive. Whether Vitality awards points for nutrition logging was not verified.

### Gaps
- Verified ZAR prices for MacroFactor, Cronometer, Carbon, RP Diet, Cal AI and Yazio could not be obtained because apps.apple.com and Play Store fetches were blocked. These should be checked manually in the ZA storefronts.
- No local South African macro-tracking apps with ZAR subscriptions were identified, and no Discovery nutrition integration was confirmed.
- Typical ZAR online macro coach pricing beyond one data point (R750/mo) was not found.

## Q3. Industry benchmarks (RevenueCat / Adapty) and purchasing-power considerations

### Takeaway
Health & Fitness is the strongest category for early monetisation and trial conversion, and the only one where annual plans dominate (about 61% of subscriptions). It also has the weakest first-year annual renewal (about 30%), so pricing should favour annual plans while retention features carry the business. The typical fitness price point is $49.99–79.99/yr or $9.99–14.99/mo.

### Cited Findings
- RevenueCat's State of Subscription Apps 2026 (March 2026) covers 115k+ apps and over $16B in revenue — [SaaStr summary](https://saastr.com/the-top-10-learnings-from-revenuecats-state-of-subscription-apps-how-115000-mobile-apps-deliver-16b-in-revenue-whats-working-whats-quietly-killing-growth); [RevenueCat report](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/) (direct fetch blocked)
- Health & Fitness leads early monetisation with a median revenue per install (RPI) of $0.48 at Day 14, rising to $0.66 at Day 60 — secondary summary via [SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/the-top-10-learnings-from-revenuecats-state-of-subscription-apps/)
- Health & Fitness is the only category where annual plans dominate (60.6%). Its first-renewal retention is 30.3%, the lowest category, versus 58.1% for Utilities. Overall first-year annual retention fell from 31% to 28% — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026 insights pt 2](https://www.revenuecat.com/sosa-2026-insights-part-2); [AppsOps mid-2026 benchmarks](https://appsops.store/news/subscription-retention-benchmarks-app-economy-mid-2026)
- Annual reactivation is only 5%, while monthly subscribers come back at 4x that rate — [9to5Mac, 27 May 2026](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/27/new-report-shows-annual-app-subscribers-rarely-return-after-they-cancel/); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/95-of-annual-app-subscribers-who-cancel-never-return-revenuecat-finds/)
- Trial-to-paid: the RevenueCat 2025 Health & Fitness median is 39.9%, and the top 10% convert at 68.3%. Adapty 2026 puts Health & Fitness at 35.0% (the highest category; Entertainment is 19.1%) — [Adapty 2026](https://adapty.io/state-of-in-app-subscriptions-2026); [RevenueCat 2025](https://revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025)
- The best Health & Fitness apps convert more than 23% of installs to trials, over 3x the median — [Social Outline](https://thesocialoutline.com/blog/install-to-trial-conversion-rate-benchmarks)
- The most common fitness-app annual price is $49.99–79.99/yr, with monthly plans at $9.99–14.99 (citing Adapty 2025) — [AppsFinboard](https://appsfinboard-landing-page.pages.dev/blog/subscription-pricing-paywall-optimization-mobile-apps/); see also [AppsOps H&F pricing 2026](https://appsops.store/blog/health-fitness-app-subscription-pricing-2026)
- Revenue is highly concentrated: about 95% of subscription revenue goes to the top 10% of apps (Adapty 2026) — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/95-of-app-subscription-revenue-goes-to-top-10-adaptys-2026-benchmark-report/)
- Plan type matters more than region for retention: there is a 4.1pp gap between the best and worst regions versus 15pp+ between plan types — [AppsOps](https://appsops.store/news/subscription-retention-benchmarks-app-economy-mid-2026)

### Inferences
- Lead with an annual plan, and price monthly at about 2x the annualised rate to anchor. Because annual churners rarely return, invest in month 10–12 renewal engagement such as progress reports and diet-phase planning.
- Region is a weak retention lever but a strong price lever. A PPP-discounted South African price should not harm retention.

### Gaps
- The RevenueCat 2026 median Health & Fitness price and trial-length distribution could not be read directly (the site was blocked). Figures are from secondary summaries.
- No numeric RevenueCat data for low-income or emerging-market price tiers was found.

## Q4. Human coaching price points (premium tier comparator)

### Takeaway
Online human nutrition and macro coaching typically costs $100–300/mo in the US, with basic packages at $65–99/mo and premium 1:1 coaching at $300–600+. Locally in South Africa, one macro or meal-plan service charges R750/mo.

### Cited Findings
- Most online nutrition coaches charge $100–300/mo per client — [Coachway](https://coachway.io/articles/how-much-do-nutrition-coaches-charge/)
- Online packages cost $99–200/mo, with basic services at $65–99/mo — [ConsumerAffairs](https://www.consumeraffairs.com/health/how-much-does-a-nutrition-coach-cost.html)
- Macro coaching with weekly check-ins costs about $150–250/mo (illustrative). Premium 1:1 coaching costs $300–600+/mo — [Coachway packages](https://coachway.io/articles/nutrition-coaching-packages/)
- Coaching software is a cost line for coaches (Trainerize-type platforms) — [QuickCoach](https://www.quickcoach.fit/cost-of-coaching-software-nutrition-coaches.html)
- Examples of individual coach listings: $100/mo — [Gumroad](https://baguaoffsite.gumroad.com/l/qrxhf); $280/mo — [Gumroad](https://soultalkcoaching.gumroad.com/l/monthly)
- South Africa: R750/mo — [The 146 Method](https://the146.vercel.app/meal-plans)

### Inferences
- There is a large price gap between algorithmic apps (about $6–12/mo effective) and human coaches ($100–300/mo). A hybrid tier at $30–60/mo (for example, AI daily support plus a human weekly or biweekly check-in) is underserved. RP Hypertrophy's $34.99/mo shows US users will pay $30+/mo for expert-branded software.

### Gaps
- The Macro Experiment's coaching and programme pricing was not found.
- Specific Trainerize-based macro coach price lists were not gathered.

## Q5. Recommendation inputs: price bands for a new IIFYM/macro coaching app

### Takeaway
(a) Software-only: about $9.99–12.99/mo and $59.99–79.99/yr in the US, and about R99–149/mo and R699–999/yr in South Africa. (b) Hybrid AI plus human: about $39–79/mo in the US and about R399–899/mo in South Africa, sitting below the $100–300 human-coach market and above the $10–20 apps.

### Cited Findings
- Adaptive "coach" competitors in software: MacroFactor $71.99/yr; Carbon $79.99–99.99/yr; Avatar $97.99/yr; RP Diet $99.99–149.99/yr; MFP Premium $79.99/yr — see the Q1 sources
- The category's common price band is $49.99–79.99/yr or $9.99–14.99/mo — [AppsFinboard](https://appsfinboard-landing-page.pages.dev/blog/subscription-pricing-paywall-optimization-mobile-apps/)
- Human coaching costs $100–300/mo — [Coachway](https://coachway.io/articles/how-much-do-nutrition-coaches-charge/). In South Africa, one example is R750/mo — [The 146 Method](https://the146.vercel.app/meal-plans)
- MyFitnessPal in ZAR: about R1,399.99/yr — [AppTail ZA](https://apptail.io/app/myfitnesspal-calorie-counter-Zr/south-africa)
- The PPP localisation guide puts $19.99 at about ZAR 163 — [PricePush](https://pricepush.app/blog/app-price-localization-cheat-sheet)

### Inferences
- (a) Software-only (adaptive TDEE, macro targets, barcode included, AI photo logging):
  - US launch at $11.99/mo or $59.99–69.99/yr, slightly under MacroFactor's $71.99. Use a 7-day trial, which is standard for the category; trial length here was not verified.
  - Consider a lifetime tier only as a capped early-adopter offer, because Health & Fitness first-year renewal is weak (about 30%).
  - South Africa: R119–149/mo and R799–999/yr. That is roughly 30–45% below MFP's ZAR annual price and broadly in line with PPP-adjusted levels.
- (b) Hybrid AI plus human coaching:
  - US: $39–59/mo for AI daily support plus an asynchronous human check-in every 2 weeks, and $79–99/mo for a weekly human check-in.
  - South Africa: R399–599/mo for the light tier and R699–899/mo for the weekly tier. This sits at or under the R750/mo local benchmark and well under US-parity coach prices.
  - Margins depend on coach-to-client ratio; this was not researched.
- Use annual-first paywalls (Health & Fitness is about 61% annual) and price monthly at 1.8–2.5x the annualised rate.
- For context, Health & Fitness median RPI is about $0.48 at Day 14. Trial conversion of 35–40% is the bar.
- All ZAR conversions assume roughly R17–18.5/USD (unverified this session) and include 15% VAT inside store prices.

### Gaps
- No direct ZA-storefront confirmation for any competitor other than the MFP aggregator capture.
- No data was found on willingness-to-pay specific to South African fitness app users.
- RevenueCat's emerging-market pricing breakdown was unavailable.
